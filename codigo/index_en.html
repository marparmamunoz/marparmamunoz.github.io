MARTINA PARMA MUÑOZ

How does the movement of the body reveal something else beyond itself? My works are explorations looking for the expression of the body when it is stripped away from any notion whatsoever; for example, what does a body look like when it moves without a clear purpose? Or, when we dive into a repetitive movement, what is then revealed to us? Therefore, I seek to find the varied expressions of the body in its purest state —and perhaps— in its most abstract form
